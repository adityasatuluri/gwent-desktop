The Objective

Gwent is a best-of-three rounds card game. To win a match, you must win two rounds. To win a round, you must have a higher Total Strength than your opponent when the round ends.

The Turn System

Players take turns placing exactly one card on the battlefield. You may play a Unit card to increase your strength, or a Special card to affect the board.

If you have no cards left, or strategically choose to stop playing, you must Pass. Once you pass, you cannot play any more cards for the rest of that round. The opponent may continue playing cards until they also pass.

Mulligan

At the start of the game, you draw 10 cards from your deck. You may discard up to 2 cards and redraw replacements to optimize your starting hand. Remember, you do not automatically draw new cards each round, so manage your hand carefully!

Northern Realms

Passive: Grants an extra card to the player if they win a round.

Specializes in Siege weapons, Spies, and Tight Bond units. A highly consistent deck with strong synergy and card advantage capabilities.

Nilfgaardian Empire

Passive: Wins any round that ends in a draw.

Features powerful Spies, strong base units, and Medics. Excellent at controlling the pace of the game and reviving fallen units.

Scoia'tael

Passive: Chooses who takes the first turn in the match.

Highly agile non-human forces with many Agile units (can be placed in Melee or Ranged) and Medics. Great at adapting to weather effects.

Monsters

Passive: Keeps one random Unit card on the board at the end of each round.

Relies on sheer numbers and overwhelming force. Uses Muster abilities to summon entire swarms of creatures from the deck instantly.

Unit Cards

Units provide Strength to your army. They are placed in one of three rows: Melee (sword), Ranged (crossbow), or Siege (trebuchet).

Hero Cards

Hero units (marked with a golden banner) are legendary figures. They cannot be affected by any Special cards, Weather effects, or abilities—neither positive nor negative.

Weather Cards

Weather cards reduce the strength of ALL non-hero units in a specific row to 1. Biting Frost affects Melee, Impenetrable Fog affects Ranged, and Torrential Rain affects Siege. Clear Weather removes all active weather effects.

Special Cards

Decoy: Swap with a non-hero unit on your board to return it to your hand. Useful for reusing Medics or stealing Spies.
Commander's Horn: Doubles the strength of all non-hero units in a specific row.
Scorch: Instantly destroys the strongest unit(s) on the entire battlefield (including yours).

Spy

Place this card on your opponent's battlefield. Its strength is added to their total, but you immediately draw 2 new cards from your deck. Essential for gaining card advantage.

Medic

When played, choose one non-hero unit from your discard pile and play it instantly. Can be chained for massive revivals.

Muster

When played, automatically finds all cards with the exact same name in your deck and hand, and plays them instantly. Highly powerful but vulnerable to Scorch.

Tight Bond

When placed next to a card with the exact same name, both cards double their strength. Three identical cards will triple their strength!

Morale Boost

Adds +1 strength to all other units in its row. Does not affect itself.
